📋 Instructions

  1. Upload your floor plan: Click the upload area or drag and drop an image
  2. Provide dimensions: Enter the approximate building dimensions
  3. Generate: Click "Generate 3D Model" to process the floor plan
  4. View: Navigate to the 3D viewer to explore your building

Note: For best results, use clear floor plan images with visible walls and rooms. The system uses computer vision to detect walls automatically. If OpenCV is not installed, you'll be prompted to provide manual dimensions.
